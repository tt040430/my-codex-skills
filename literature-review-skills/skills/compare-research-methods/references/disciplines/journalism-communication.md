# Journalism and Communication / 新闻传播学

## Scope and Disambiguation

Use for journalism, communication, media studies, news production, audience research and platform-mediated communication. Distinguish scholarly literature about news from news reports used as primary material. A report describing an event does not establish a research consensus.

## Question and Concepts

Identify the unit: news text/image, journalist, newsroom, audience member, platform, institution or media system. Separate perceived credibility, trust in institutions, exposure, engagement and behavior. Do not infer audience effects from text frequency or likes alone.

Possible lenses include framing, agenda-setting, gatekeeping, professional roles and platform governance. Select a lens only if it clarifies the question; verify theoretical sources before attributing claims. Conceptual histories and interpretive reviews need bounded concepts, periods and source traditions rather than compulsory independent/dependent variables.

## Search Plan

- Candidate sources: [Communication & Mass Media Complete](https://www.ebsco.com/products/research-databases/communication-mass-media-complete), multidisciplinary indexes, and CNKI/万方/维普 via available institutional access. Record actual access, query, fields, dates and exclusions. CSSCI is an indexing boundary, not a review method; record the edition/year if used as a filter.
- Term families: 新闻可信度 / news credibility; 算法新闻 / automated journalism; 生成式人工智能 / generative AI; 来源披露 / source disclosure; 新闻回避 / news avoidance. Expand from the topic, not from this list mechanically.
- Illustrative Boolean seed, not an executed search: `(journalis* OR "news production") AND ("generative AI" OR "automated journalism") AND (credibility OR trust OR disclosure)`. Adapt wildcard, phrase and field syntax to each database and test against known relevant papers.
- Keep news texts, policy statements and industry reports in a separate primary/context corpus. Record language and geographic coverage; do not label English-only coverage as worldwide coverage.

## Extraction and Appraisal

Add platform/outlet, country/language, period, unit, theoretical construct, operational measure, sampling and material type to the common evidence table.

| Design | Inspect | Inference boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Content/frame analysis | Sampling frame, coding unit, codebook, coder agreement and disputed categories | Text patterns do not establish audience effects |
| Audience survey | Recruitment, measurement validity, self-report and confounding | Cross-sectional association is not causal change |
| Experiment | Randomization, stimulus realism, manipulation checks and outcome timing | Results may not transfer to routine media use |
| Interviews/ethnography | Access, context, reflexivity, analytical procedure and counterexamples | Do not generalize prevalence from purposive accounts |
| Computational text/network study | API coverage, missing/deleted content, model validation and network definitions | Platform traces do not represent all audiences |
| Historical/discourse analysis | Corpus selection, interpretive warrant, context and competing readings | Do not grade it by causal identification criteria |

## Synthesis and Gap

Possible modules: production practices; content and disclosure; audience interpretation; institutional/platform conditions. Choose axes based on the question, and allow a paper to contribute to several modules with distinct claims.

Compare conflicting results only after checking whether studies measure the same kind of trust, use comparable stimuli and examine similar populations. Treat context explanations as hypotheses until supported. Challenge a gap with: would it remain if the outcome measure, platform or language corpus changed?

## Interview Prompts

- 你说的“信任”是对单条新闻、媒体机构，还是 AI 工具的信任？
- 你准备综述新闻生产过程，还是受众接收效果？
- 内容分析能支持你想写的“影响受众”结论吗？还缺哪类证据？
- 哪个相反结果会让你修改目前的解释？

## Planning Example (No Literature Claims)

Topic: AI-generated news. Candidate anchor: under which disclosure and source conditions do audiences evaluate AI-generated news as credible? Begin by choosing the outcome and audience, then map studies by disclosure design, source cues and measurement. No supplied papers means no claims about what prior studies found.
